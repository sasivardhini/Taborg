### 🗂️ Taborg - Professional Tab & Window Management

Take control of your browser tabs and boost your productivity with Taborg, the modern tab organizer designed for professionals, developers, students, and anyone who works with multiple tabs.

**Never lose important tabs. Always stay organized.**

---

### ✨ KEY FEATURES

**📁 Smart Tab Grouping**
Organize your tabs into color-coded groups with custom names. Perfect for separating work projects, research topics, or personal browsing.

**💾 Session Save & Restore**
Save your current browsing session with one click and restore it anytime. Never lose your work when closing the browser. Great for:
- Daily work routines
- Research projects
- Shopping lists
- Study sessions

**🔍 Instant Tab Search**
Find any tab instantly by searching titles or URLs. No more endless scrolling through dozens of tabs.

**🔄 Duplicate Tab Detector**
Automatically detect and remove duplicate tabs. Save memory and reduce clutter with one click.

**🧹 One-Click Cleanup**
Close all inactive tabs instantly while protecting your active and pinned tabs. Keep your browser fast and organized.

**⚡ Keyboard Shortcuts**
Work faster with built-in shortcuts:
- Ctrl+Shift+T: Open Taborg
- Ctrl+Shift+S: Quick save session
- Ctrl+Shift+D: Remove duplicates

**📊 Usage Statistics**
Track your productivity with stats on tabs closed, duplicates removed, and sessions saved.

## Permissions Justification

**Required Permissions:**

1. **tabs**
   - **Reason**: To read and display tab titles, URLs, and favicons in the extension interface
   - **Usage**: Accessing tab information for display and management

2. **storage**
   - **Reason**: To save user sessions and preferences locally
   - **Usage**: Storing saved sessions and extension settings on the user's device

3. **tabGroups**
   - **Reason**: To create and manage tab groups
   - **Usage**: Enabling the tab grouping feature when users organize tabs

4. **Host permission: `<all_urls>`**
   - **Reason**: To access favicons and page titles from any website
   - **Usage**: Displaying tab information accurately in the extension UI

**Privacy Note**: All data is stored locally. No data is transmitted to external servers.

## Testing Instructions (for review team)

**How to Test:**

1. **Basic Functionality**
   - Install extension
   - Click icon to open popup
   - Verify all tabs are displayed

2. **Tab Grouping**
   - Click "Create Group"
   - Select tabs, choose color, enter name
   - Verify group is created in browser

3. **Session Save/Restore**
   - Click "Save Session"
   - Enter name and save
   - Close tabs
   - Click "Restore" on saved session
   - Verify tabs reopen in new window

4. **Duplicate Detection**
   - Open same URL in multiple tabs
   - Open extension
   - Verify duplicates are marked
   - Click "Remove Duplicates"
   - Verify duplicates are closed

5. **Search**
   - Type in search box
   - Verify tabs filter in real-time

6. **Keyboard Shortcuts**
   - Press Ctrl+Shift+S (save session)
   - Press Ctrl+Shift+D (remove duplicates)
   - Verify actions work

**Test Account**: Not required
